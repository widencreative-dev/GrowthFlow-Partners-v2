Built from both sides of the table.

Twenty years across finance, investing, operations, and entrepreneurship.

GFlow was built from experience evaluating businesses, operating, and helping them grow. That perspective started in institutional finance and private equity, with work spanning investment analysis, treasury, forecasting, and capital allocation, and later moved directly into operating businesses where financial decisions had to translate into real-world execution.

Over time, that experience expanded across startups, manufacturing, construction, healthcare, professional services, and sports-related organizations. Different industries, but many of the same challenges: managing cash through growth, understanding margins, building financial discipline, making better investment decisions, and creating systems that can keep up as the business becomes more complex.

Today, GFlow brings those perspectives together to help owners and management teams understand what is happening in the business, anticipate what is coming next, and make stronger decisions around growth, capital, profitability, and execution.

GFlow combines both. Most CFOs haven't operated, and most operators don't have PE experience.
